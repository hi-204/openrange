Your seat relative to the button, your [[position]], changes which hands are worth playing.
Two players holding the same cards in different seats should often play them differently.

## The six seats

In 6-max, from the first to act preflop to the last:

| Seat | Name | Note |
|---|---|---|
| UTG | [[Under the gun]] | first to act preflop |
| HJ | Hijack | |
| CO | Cutoff | the seat before the button |
| BTN | Button | acts last on every street after the flop |
| SB | Small blind | acts first on every street after the flop |
| BB | Big blind | acts last preflop |

## Why acting last helps

After the flop the order is fixed: the blinds act first and the button last. A player who acts
after their opponent is [[in position]]; the one who acts first is [[out of position]].

Acting last means:

- **You see their decision before you make yours.** A check or a bet tells you something.
- **You control the size of the pot.** You can check behind to see a free card, or bet.
- **You make better use of your cards.** Hands play more easily when you aren't guessing first.

## Why early seats open fewer hands

Preflop, the first player to act has five players behind who could wake up with a strong hand.
The button has only two (the blinds) and will have position on both of them after the flop.
That is why ranges widen from UTG to the button, as the charts in lesson 1.5 show.

```callout key
Two things make a seat good: few players left to act behind you, and acting last after the
flop. The button has both.
```

## Worked hand

You hold K♣ J♦.

- **From UTG.** Five players act after you. Any of them may hold A-K, A-J, K-Q or a big pair,
  hands that dominate yours. If someone calls from a later seat, they will act after you on
  every street.
- **From the button.** Only the blinds remain, they hold random hands most of the time, and you
  will act last after the flop whoever calls.

Same cards, very different situations: K-J is a comfortable open from the button and a risky
one from UTG.

## Common mistakes

- **Playing the same hands from every seat.** Position changes a hand's value.
- **Thinking the big blind is a good seat because it acts last preflop.** After the flop it
  acts before everyone except the small blind.
- **Ignoring who is still to act.** Count the players behind you before you open.
